An internal combustion engine consists of several major parts, each playing a crucial role in power generation. With VR, learners can explore 3D models of engines, view exploded diagrams, and interact with components to understand their structure and function.<br><br>

<b>Main parts of an engine include:</b><br>
- Cylinder – The chamber where combustion occurs.<br>
- Piston – Moves up and down to transfer force from expanding gases.<br>
- Connecting Rod – Connects piston to crankshaft, transmitting motion.<br>
- Crankshaft – Converts reciprocating motion of piston into rotary motion.<br>
- Valves (Inlet & Exhaust) – Control entry of air-fuel mixture and exit of exhaust gases.<br>
- Camshaft – Operates valves in synchronization with crankshaft.<br>
- Spark Plug / Fuel Injector – Ignites fuel-air mixture (petrol) or injects fuel (diesel).<br>
- Flywheel – Maintains rotational inertia for smooth running.<br><br>

<b>Advantages of using VR for engine study:</b><br>
- Safe and cost-effective (no need to dismantle real engines).<br>
- Interactive 3D visualization of hidden/complex parts.<br>
- Ability to simulate working cycles dynamically.<br>
- Repeatable learning without physical wear and tear.<br>
