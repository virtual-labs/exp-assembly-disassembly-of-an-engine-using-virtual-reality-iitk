1. Launch the VR Engine Parts Simulator.<br>
2. Wear VR headset and calibrate controllers.<br>
3. Select engine model type (Petrol 4-stroke, Diesel 4-stroke, etc.).<br>
4. Explore engine components in exploded view.<br>
5. Interact with each part to learn: shape and placement, material used, function in engine cycle.<br>
6. Simulate engine operation (intake, compression, power, exhaust).<br>
7. Observe motion of piston, crankshaft, valves, and fuel system.<br>
8. Record observations and note differences between petrol & diesel engines.
